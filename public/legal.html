Terms, privacy and risk.

The notices that govern a vault at Hedgestone Finance.

Privacy notice

Hedgestone Finance collects the information needed to open and run your vault: your name, address, date of birth, Social Security number, identification documents, what goes into and out of your vault, and the devices you use to reach us. We share it only with the service providers who help us run the platform, and with regulators and law enforcement where the law requires it. We do not sell it.

You may limit marketing at any time under Alerts once you are signed in, or by calling our support team.

Terms of use

Access to your vault is personal to you. Keep your password and one-time codes to yourself, tell us immediately if you think someone else has them, and review your activity. We may hold, delay or decline a request to send Bitcoin where we reasonably suspect fraud, where it would breach a limit, or where a manual check is required. Where we decline one, the Bitcoin stays in your vault and you are told why.

Everything added to your vault is booked at Bitcoin’s price on the day the payment was made or the Bitcoin arrived. Each investment carries the term you chose, from 10 to 100 years, and a maturity date; it can be sent out of your vault only once that date has passed.

Risk warning

Bitcoin is a volatile asset. Its price can fall as well as rise, sometimes sharply, and you could get back less than you invest. Past growth is not a guide to future growth. Invest only what you can afford to leave invested. The growth shown on your dashboard is calculated from the market price and is not a forecast. Bitcoin in your vault is not a deposit and is not covered by any deposit insurance scheme.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA across the site and your vault. If any part of it is difficult to use, tell us on our support line or support@hedgestonesfinance.com and we will help you complete what you were doing and fix the underlying problem.

About this application

Hedgestone Finance is a fictional company. This site is a demonstration of a Bitcoin vault application: the vaults, balances and customer records used throughout are synthetic, and no real money or Bitcoin moves through it.
