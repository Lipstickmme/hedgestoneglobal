How it works

Three steps, decades apart.

1

Open your vault

Tell us about yourself and someone from our team verifies your identity and opens your vault, usually the same business day.

2

Add Bitcoin, set the term

Pay cash through our OTC partner or send Bitcoin you hold, and choose how long it stays: 10 to 100 years. It is priced on the day it goes in.

3

Follow it to maturity

Your dashboard charts its value every day and counts down to its maturity date. From that day it can be sent to a wallet of yours.
