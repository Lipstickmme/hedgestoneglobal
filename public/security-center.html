How we protect your vault, and how you can.

What runs on our side, what to switch on for yours, and what to do the moment something looks wrong.

We will never ask for a one-time code. Not by phone, not by text, not by email. If anyone asks for a code or your password, hang up and call our security line.

What runs whether you think about it or not.

Every send checked

Bitcoin leaves a vault only after our team has checked the request against what has matured.

Encrypted at rest

Social Security numbers and identity documents are encrypted with keys the application cannot print.

Every action logged

Who did what, from which device and address. You can read your own history under Activity.

Sessions you control

See every signed-in device and sign any of them out, from anywhere.

Four things worth ten minutes.

  1. Turn on two-step verification, and leave it on.
  2. Set a PIN for sending Bitcoin that you use nowhere else.
  3. Only send Bitcoin to the vault address on your Receive page, and check it every time.
  4. Check your own wallet address twice before you ask us to send to it.
