What you are buying

A share of a Bitcoin, at the live price.

Bitcoin divides into a hundred million parts, so $1 buys a real amount of it. When you confirm, we book the purchase at the live price shown on your screen and the Bitcoin appears in your account straight away, to the satoshi.

  • Bitcoin is the only asset we invest in. There is nothing else to choose between.
  • No commission to buy or sell. The price you see is the price you pay.
  • If the price moves more than 2% while you decide, we show you the new one and ask again.
