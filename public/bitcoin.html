How the vault works

Bitcoin, held for the long term.

How Bitcoin goes into your vault, how it is priced, and how it comes back to you when it matures.

Two ways in

Cash or Bitcoin, priced on the day it arrives.

However it comes in, each investment is booked at Bitcoin’s price on the day it was made, so the growth you see is the growth since that day.

  • Pay cash through our OTC partner. Pay from a bank in your name, quoting your reference. When the partner confirms, the payment buys Bitcoin at that day’s price.
  • Send Bitcoin you already hold. Send it to your vault address. When it arrives it is added at its value that day.
  • Held to the satoshi. Bitcoin divides into a hundred million parts, so every amount is exact.
