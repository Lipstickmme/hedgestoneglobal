Business banking · Columbus, OH

Meridian Logistics

Meridian ran payroll through a processor that took three business days to settle, which meant funding on Tuesday for a Friday pay date. Moving origination onto Rockfield same-day ACH, with dual approval held by their controller and CFO, brought funding to the day before and removed the float they were carrying purely to cover the gap.
